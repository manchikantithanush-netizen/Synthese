5.3 Device Permissions
The App requests the following device permissions to function properly. Each permission is requested in context, with an in-app explanation, before access is granted. You may deny or revoke any permission through your device settings at any time; revoking certain permissions will limit corresponding features of the App.

Network & Connectivity
•       INTERNET — Required to connect to Firebase (Authentication, Firestore, and Storage), Google Maps, the App’s AI nutrition backend, and to fetch updates within the App.

Camera & Media
•       CAMERA — Used to take photos directly within the App (for example, profile pictures and food photos for nutritional analysis) and to measure your heart rate by placing your finger over the camera.
•       Photo selection — When you choose an existing photo from your device, the App uses the Android System Photo Picker (on Android 13 and above), which lets you share only the specific photos you select and requires no broad gallery permission.
•       READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE — Provides gallery access only on devices running Android 12 or below (restricted to those devices via maxSdkVersion=“32”). The App does not request READ_MEDIA_IMAGES or broad media access on newer devices.

Physical Activity & Step Tracking
•       ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION — Required by the step-counting feature to access the device’s built-in step sensor and track your daily steps.

Location
•       ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION — Provides approximate location data, used by the mapping features within the App.
•       ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — Provides precise GPS location, used for accurate location tracking on the in-app map during an active, user-initiated workout session.

The App does NOT request background location access. Location is accessed only while you are actively using a location-based feature in the foreground.

Foreground Services
•       FOREGROUND_SERVICE — Required to run a user-visible foreground service (StepMilestoneService) that enables all-day step tracking with a persistent notification, so your steps are counted even when the App is not on screen.
•       FOREGROUND_SERVICE_HEALTH — Declares that the step-tracking foreground service accesses health-related data (step counts), as required for foreground services of type “health” on Android 14 and above. This is backed by the ACTIVITY_RECOGNITION permission.
•       FOREGROUND_SERVICE_LOCATION — Declares that location may be used by a foreground service, as required on Android 14 and above, when you run a location-based workout session.

Notifications & Background Restart
•       POST_NOTIFICATIONS — Required on Android 13 and above to display notifications, used to alert you to step milestones and health reminders. Notifications are service-related only and are never used for marketing.
•       RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED — Allows the App to restart its step-tracking service automatically after your device reboots, ensuring uninterrupted activity tracking.

Device Function
•       WAKE_LOCK — Used to keep the screen and processor active during a heart-rate measurement so the reading can complete.
•       VIBRATE — Used for haptic feedback within the App.

Health Connect
Synthese does not currently integrate with Google Health Connect. If this changes in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly to describe what Health Connect data is accessed, how it is used, and the permissions required.

5.5 AI-Powered Nutritional Analysis
Synthese uses artificial intelligence to estimate nutritional information based on input you provide. Specifically:
•       When you type a meal or food name, that text is sent to an AI model to generate estimated nutritional data (such as calories, macronutrients, and portion information).
•       When you take or select a photo of food, that image is sent to an AI model which analyses the visual content to identify the food and estimate its nutritional values.

How this data is routed
Your food input (text and/or food images) is first transmitted over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection to our own secure backend service, which then forwards it to GitHub Models (powered by OpenAI’s GPT-4o mini) solely to generate the nutritional estimate. This transmission is necessary only to deliver the nutrition feature and is subject to GitHub’s and OpenAI’s applicable data processing terms.

We do not transmit your name, email, account credentials, or any other personally identifying information to this AI service or our backend for this purpose. Only the food-related input (text description or photo) is sent, and it is used solely to return a nutritional estimate — it is not used to train models or for any commercial purpose.

Important Limitations
AI-generated nutritional information is an estimate only. Results may not be fully accurate and should not be relied upon for medical, clinical, or dietary treatment purposes. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional or registered dietitian for personalised nutritional advice.
